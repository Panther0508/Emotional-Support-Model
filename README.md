# Emotional Support AI 🤖💛

A **startup-ready, self-learning AI chatbot** that provides **empathetic emotional support**. Built with **Python**, **Streamlit**, and **TextBlob**, this AI detects user emotions, responds with personalized messages, provides coping suggestions, and tracks conversations over time. Open-source and free to use.  

---

## 🌟 Features

- **Granular Emotion Detection**: Detects multiple emotions including *happy, sad, anxious, angry, neutral*.  
- **Adaptive Responses**: Learns from past conversations for better, personalized support.  
- **Multi-User Support**: Each user has their own conversation log stored separately.  
- **Custom AI Personality**: Choose AI personality: *empathetic, funny, motivational*.  
- **Coping Suggestions**: Provides quick tips for stress, sadness, anxiety, or anger.  
- **Conversation History & Analytics**: View recent conversations and track emotional trends.  
- **Export & Journal**: Save your conversations for personal reflection (JSON format).  
- **Startup-ready MVP**: Ideal for portfolio, demo, or further expansion.
